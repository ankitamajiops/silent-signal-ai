About SILENT SIGNAL AI

SILENT SIGNAL AI identifies potential information blind spots affecting communities during disasters by examining missing updates, communication disruptions and essential-service gaps.

Methodology

  • Collect community status reports.
  • Examine communication and essential-service indicators.
  • Calculate transparent, rule-based risk scores.
  • Prioritize locations for human verification.

Important Limitations

This is a demonstration prototype using sample data. Silence alone does not establish that a community is in danger. All flagged situations require human verification.
